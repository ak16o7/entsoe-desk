A86 · Imbalance volume iWhat is A86?
ENTSO-E Total Imbalance Volume is the aggregated system imbalance for each imbalance settlement period (ISP / MTU). It is an energy volume in MWh, not an instantaneous MW level.

In the source data, surplus/excess and deficit are distinguished. This dashboard keeps the sign returned/derived from the ENTSO-E series: positive indicates surplus/excess and negative indicates deficit when the source uses that convention.

This is the system imbalance that remained to be settled; it is not the same thing as the amount of balancing energy activated.Latest MTU: —
A83 · Net activation iWhat is A83?
ENTSO-E A83 is activated balancing energy by reserve type and direction. Here the dashboard requests aFRR (A96) and mFRR (A97) across the four German control areas and nets Up minus Down.

Positive means net upward activation: more generation / less consumption was activated. Negative means net downward activation: less generation / more consumption was activated.

A83 is shown in MW per balancing time unit. It tells you what balancing action was activated, whereas A86 tells you the resulting imbalance volume.Latest MTU: —
